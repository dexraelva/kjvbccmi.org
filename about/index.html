About Our Church

A Bible-believing, Christ-centered church committed to the faithful preaching of God’s Word and the spiritual growth of believers.

Who We Are

A church family built on faith, truth, and service.

KJV Bible Christian Church and Ministries Inc. is committed to the Gospel of Jesus Christ, the authority of Scripture, and the work of the local church.

Our desire is to glorify God by preaching the Word, discipling believers, strengthening families, encouraging prayer, and serving others with humility and love.

  • Christ-centered worship and preaching
  • Biblical discipleship and spiritual growth
  • Family-focused fellowship and service
  • Local and mission-minded outreach
🎯

Our Mission

To proclaim the Gospel of Jesus Christ, teach sound doctrine, equip believers for service, and reach communities with the love and truth of God’s Word.

🌿

Our Vision

To see lives transformed by the Word of God and to build a faithful community of believers who serve God, love one another, and reach the lost.

What We Believe

Core beliefs that guide our church.

This section is prepared in a simple format so visitors can understand the foundation of the ministry.

📖

The Bible

We believe the Bible is the Word of God and the final authority for faith and life.

✝️

Salvation

We believe salvation is by grace through faith in Jesus Christ and His finished work.

🙏

Prayer

We believe prayer is essential in the life of every believer and local church.

⛪

The Local Church

We believe the local church is God’s place for worship, fellowship, service, and discipleship.

🤝

Discipleship

We believe believers should grow in doctrine, character, service, and spiritual maturity.

🌏

Evangelism

We believe the church is called to share the Gospel and support missions.
