Trust & Stewardship

Keep the donation page clear and official.

For a real church website, donation information should be verified and approved by the church leadership before publishing. Avoid posting personal account details unless officially authorized.

  • Use official church payment accounts
  • Add QR codes only from verified sources
  • Give donors a way to confirm their donation
  • Keep financial wording respectful and not forceful
